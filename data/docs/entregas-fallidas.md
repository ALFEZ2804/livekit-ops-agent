# Failed deliveries

## What is a failed delivery

A failed delivery is a delivery attempt that is not completed. The most common causes are
the recipient not being at home, an incorrect or incomplete address, and the recipient
refusing the parcel at the moment of delivery. The driver records the reason in the app and the order
moves to an exception status.

## Retry attempts

Nordport makes up to three attempts to deliver a parcel on consecutive business days. After
the first failed attempt we notify the recipient by SMS and email with the reason and the
date of the next attempt. If the second attempt also fails, the recipient can choose a third
attempt or pick the parcel up at a nearby convenience point.

## Recipient notification

Each failed attempt generates an automatic notification with a link to reschedule the
delivery, change the address or authorize delivery to a neighbor. The notification includes
the order number and the estimated window for the next delivery.

## Return to sender

If all three attempts fail and the recipient does not collect the parcel at the convenience
point within seven calendar days, the order is returned to the sender. The return to origin
is free for the recipient, but it may have a cost for the sender depending on the service
contract.
