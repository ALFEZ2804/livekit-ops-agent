# Delivery times and windows

## Transit times

Nordport's standard service delivers within two or three business days in the domestic
network. The express service delivers on the next business day if the order is registered
before the cut-off. Remote regions and islands add between one and three extra business days.

## Cut-off time

The cut-off time for same-day dispatch is 2 PM. An order registered and collected before
2 PM on a business day enters that day's route; after that time it counts as the next
business day. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays do not count as business days.

## Delivery windows

Last-mile delivery is organized in two windows: the morning window, from 9 AM to 2 PM, and
the afternoon window, from 4 PM to 8 PM. The recipient gets a narrower estimated slot on the
delivery day itself. Windows are indicative and can vary with volume or incidents on the
route.

## Delays

A storm, a road closure or a volume peak can delay delivery beyond the estimated date. When
that happens, the order's estimated date is updated and the recipient receives a notification
with the new estimate.
