# Rescheduling a delivery

## When you can reschedule

A delivery can be rescheduled as long as the order has not already been delivered or returned
to the sender. You can move the date earlier or later, change the time window, or provide a new
address within the same city. Orders held in customs cannot be rescheduled until they are
cleared.

## How to reschedule

The recipient reschedules from the link in the delivery notification, from the customer area
using the order number, or by calling the operations team. When rescheduling, you pick an
available business day and a morning or afternoon time window. The new appointment replaces
the previous one and is confirmed with a message.

## Deadline to reschedule

A reschedule must be requested before 8 PM on the day before delivery so it enters the next
day's route planning. A later request is applied to the first business day with an available
slot.

## Limits

The same delivery can be rescheduled at most twice. After that the order waits for pickup at
a convenience point or is returned to the sender under the failed-delivery rules.
